The artist

Painting worlds
that live between breaths

I am Shambhavi Kulkarni — a painter drawn to the quiet intensity of colour and form. My work explores the boundary between the seen and the felt, crafting visual spaces that invite you to linger and discover.

Rooted in classical technique yet unafraid of abstraction, each painting is a journey — from first sketch to final glazing — a meditation on light, emotion, and the stories our eyes tell.

My approach

The process

Every painting runs its own course, but the rhythm is always the same: notice, build, and know when to stop.

01

Conception

Every work begins in silence — in a walk, a dream, a conversation half-remembered. I sketch compulsively, filling journals with gestures and colour notes until the image clarifies itself.

02

Construction

Layer by layer the painting builds. Underpainting establishes the tonal architecture. Then colour arrives in glazes — translucent, luminous — each pass revealing what the last concealed.

03

Completion

The final stage is as much about restraint as addition. Knowing when a painting is finished — trusting the silence — is the hardest and most important part of the practice.

Bespoke works

Commission your painting

Every commission is a collaboration. We begin with a conversation about the room, the light and the story the painting should hold — then I paint it, and we refine it together.

  • A private consultation, in person or over video
  • Two progress reviews with photographs at each stage
  • Your choice of medium, palette and dimensions
  • Signed certificate of authenticity on delivery

Good to know

Questions, answered

How does a commission actually begin?

With a conversation — usually 30–45 minutes, in the studio or over video. We talk about the space, the feeling you want, and any references you love. I then send a written proposal with a sketch direction, size, medium, timeline and price before anything is started.

What does a commission cost?

Pricing depends on size, medium and complexity, and is quoted in writing before work begins. A 30% deposit reserves your slot in the studio calendar; the balance is due on approval, before shipping.

Can I see the painting while it's in progress?

Yes — that's part of the process. You'll receive photographs at two agreed stages, and we can make adjustments to palette and composition at each review. Once the final layer is on, changes to the composition are no longer possible.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes. Works ship rolled in a rigid tube or crated, fully insured, with tracking. International buyers are responsible for any local duties and taxes. Shipping is quoted with your commission.

Are prints or giclée editions available?

Limited archival prints are released occasionally for sold works. Join the studio list by writing to studio@shambhavikulkarni.art and I'll let you know when the next edition opens.
